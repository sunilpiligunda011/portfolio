Philosophy

How I build

Use what is already moving.

Before adding vehicles or warehouses, look for capacity that is already in motion. BusBox starts from journeys that happen anyway.

Let the data argue first.

Loss prevention work taught me that patterns are more honest than opinions. I form a view, then look for the evidence that could break it.

Earn trust at every handover.

When parcels pass between people, reliability is the product. Verification, transparency and clear accountability are designed in from the start.

Build the real thing.

I chose to build BusBox as working software from day one instead of a clickable prototype. Real constraints show up early, and so do real answers.

BusBox

Closer than you think.

Every day, buses and travellers cross India with room to spare. BusBox turns that room into a parcel network.

BusBox is a technology-driven parcel mobility platform. A sender hands a parcel to a traveller or transport operator who is already going their way, and it rides along to its destination. The network grows with the journeys people already make, without a fleet of its own.

Coverage is designed to be anywhere to anywhere across India. If a journey connects a pickup point and a drop point, BusBox can put it to work.

What I'm working on

Product vision

A parcel layer on top of India's travel

BusBox treats journeys people already plan as delivery capacity. The goal is anywhere-to-anywhere delivery across India, without building a fleet.

Business strategy

Two sides, one network

Senders get affordable, flexible delivery. Travellers and transport operators earn from space they are already carrying. Prices follow parcel size and distance on a taper, so the total rises with distance while the rate per kilometre falls, because the trip happens anyway. Long routes are where BusBox is strongest.

Technology development

Real software from day one

One Flutter app runs sender, traveller and admin modes. A NestJS and TypeORM backend on Neon Postgres sits behind it, with a React admin console for operations and Mapbox for routes.

  • Flutter
  • NestJS
  • TypeORM
  • Neon Postgres
  • Vercel
  • React + Vite
  • Mapbox
  • Razorpay, integrating
  • SMS OTP, planned

Operational planning

One operating model for every route

The operating plan covers who can carry, how parcels change hands, how delivery is confirmed and what happens when a journey changes. It is designed to work the same way between any pickup point and drop point.

Customer experience

Clear at every handover

Each person sees what they need: the price, who has the parcel and when it will arrive. Live tracking and OTP confirmation for recipients are being built for launch.

  • Sender mode
  • Traveller mode
  • Admin mode
  • Recipient OTP, in build

Route matching

The engine at the centre

A journey qualifies when it passes the pickup and the drop-off, in that order, within the sender's time window. Matching on journeys that already exist is what keeps delivery affordable.

  • Passes the pickup
  • Passes the drop-off
  • Right order
  • Within the time window

Logistics innovation

Capacity that already moves

Most delivery networks grow by adding vehicles. BusBox grows by adding parcels to journeys already on the road.
